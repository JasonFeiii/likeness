01 · Chain reaction

k = 1

Each fission frees about 2.43 neutrons. If exactly one of them, on average, causes the next fission, the chain holds. Fewer and it dies; more and it grows every generation.

每次裂变平均释放约 2.43 个中子。平均恰好一个引发下一次裂变，链式反应就稳定；少于一个会熄灭，多于一个则逐代增长。

About 0.65% of the neutrons from uranium-235 arrive seconds late. That delay is the only reason a reactor can be steered.

铀-235 裂变中约 0.65% 的中子会晚几秒出现。正是这点延迟，让反应堆可以被控制。

02 · Cherenkov radiation

cos θ = 1/nβ

In water, light moves at 75% of its speed in vacuum. An electron that outruns it throws off a cone of light: a sonic boom made of photons.

光在水中的速度只有真空中的 75%。跑得比它更快的电子会拖出一道光锥，像是由光组成的音爆。

Emission scales as 1/λ², so short wavelengths dominate and the glow is blue. Below 0.264 MeV an electron goes dark.

辐射强度与波长平方成反比，短波长占主导，所以是蓝色。电子能量低于 0.264 MeV 时，光就熄灭了。

03 · Gamma spectroscopy

661.7 keV

Caesium-137 signs its name with one gamma line. Count photons one at a time and the line grows out of the noise, its error shrinking as 1/√N.

铯-137 用一条伽马谱线签下自己的名字。逐个光子计数，谱线从噪声里长出来，误差按 1/√N 缩小。

04 · Full scale

3.6 R/h

26 April 1986, Chernobyl unit 4. The dosimeters at hand read no higher than 3.6 roentgen per hour, so 3.6 is what they showed. Near the core the real field was thousands of times higher.

1986 年 4 月 26 日，切尔诺贝利 4 号机组。手边的剂量计量程上限是 3.6 伦琴/小时，于是它们只能显示 3.6。堆芯附近的真实场强高出数千倍。

Film shot at the site came back flecked with white: radiation striking the emulsion.

现场拍摄的胶片布满白色斑点，那是辐射直接击中感光层留下的痕迹。

06 · Confinement

100 / 24 110

In 2016 a steel arch 257 metres across was slid over unit 4, built to last a hundred years. The plutonium-239 inside takes 24,110 years to fall by half.

2016 年，一座跨度 257 米的钢拱被平移覆盖到 4 号机组上方，设计寿命 100 年。里面的钚-239 需要 24,110 年才衰减一半。
